Overview

What Lyria 4 is used for

Lyria 4 is an AI music generator for users who want a shorter path from idea or lyrics to usable audio output. The strongest public use cases are text-to-music generation, lyrics-to-song conversion, browser preview, and export-ready audio creation. Whether you are a content creator, songwriter, or commercial producer, Lyria 4 provides a browser-based environment for generating original music from descriptive text prompts or provided lyrics.

Workflow

How Lyria 4 works

1

Write a prompt or paste lyrics

Describe the song style, mood, and genre, or provide lyrics as the starting point for generation.

2

Preview the audio draft

Generate a draft result and preview it directly in the browser before deciding on next steps.

3

Refine and export

Adjust the prompt, compare multiple versions, and export the strongest result in your preferred audio format.

FAQ

Common Lyria 4 questions

Is Lyria 4 a web app?

Yes. The official site is https://www.lyria4.org and it works entirely in the browser.

Does Lyria 4 support text-to-music?

Yes. Text-to-music is one of the main public workflows — describe the style, mood, and genre you want.

Does Lyria 4 support lyrics-to-song?

Yes. You can paste your lyrics and Lyria 4 will generate a full song arrangement around them.

Is there a free plan?

Yes. Lyria 4 offers a free tier with 10 yearly credits for testing text-to-music and lyrics-to-song generation.

What audio formats can I export?

Lyria 4 supports standard audio export formats including MP3 and WAV depending on your plan.

Where can I check pricing?

Visit the official pricing page at https://www.lyria4.org/pricing for current plan details.
